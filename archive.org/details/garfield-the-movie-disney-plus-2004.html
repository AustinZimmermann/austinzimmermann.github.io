Garfield: The Movie (2004) is a live-action/CGI family comedy directed by Peter Hewitt and based on Jim Davis’s classic comic strip. Preserved here from the high-definition Disney+ streaming master, the film follows America’s favorite lasagna-loving, Monday-hating orange tabby cat (voiced by Bill Murray).

Garfield’s idyllic, pampered life with his doting owner Jon Arbuckle (Breckin Meyer) is turned upside down when Jon adopts Odie, a lovable, dim-witted dog, at the encouragement of veterinarian Dr. Liz Wilson (Jennifer Love Hewitt). When Odie is kidnapped by greedy television personality Happy Chapman (Stephen Tobolowsky), Garfield must leave his comfortable couch behind to mount a rescue mission across the city.
