## Movie Synopsis
When an experimental space voyage led by visionary scientist Reed Richards encounters a cosmic radiation storm, the five-person crew suffers profound genetic alterations. Upon returning to Earth, each member develops superhuman capabilities:

- **Reed Richards** (*Ioan Gruffudd*): Gains the ability to stretch and contort his body into extraordinary forms as **Mr. Fantastic**.
- **Sue Storm** (*Jessica Alba*): Obtains the power of invisibility and force-field creation as the **Invisible Woman**.
- **Johnny Storm** (*Chris Evans*): Master of fire and flight, capable of bursting into flame as the **Human Torch**.
- **Ben Grimm** (*Michael Chiklis*): Transformed into a rock-skinned powerhouse of immense strength as **The Thing**.

Together, they form the **Fantastic Four**, instant media sensations tasked with protecting New York City. However, their mission sponsor, industrialist Victor Von Doom (*Julian McMahon*), undergoes a sinister transformation of his own into the metallic villain **Dr. Doom**, forcing the newly formed team to unite and stop him from destroying the city.
