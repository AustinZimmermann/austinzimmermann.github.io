## Film Overview

***The Transporter*** is a high-octane 2002 martial arts action thriller directed by **Corey Yuen** and **Louis Leterrier**, written by **Luc Besson** and **Robert Mark Kamen**, starring **Jason Statham** in his star-making lead action role.

### Premise
Frank Martin (Jason Statham) is an ex-Special Forces operative who operates as a high-stakes “transporter” in the French Riviera—delivering packages for mysterious clientele without asking questions. Frank lives by a strict set of personal rules:

1. **Never change the deal.**
2. **No names.**
3. **Never look in the package.**

When hired by an American mercenary known only as “Wall Street” (Matt Schulze) to deliver a heavy package, Frank breaks Rule #3 after discovering the package is alive: containing a bound and gagged woman named Lai (Shu Qi). His decision triggers a lethal cat-and-mouse game involving human trafficking, high-speed car chases, and explosive martial arts confrontations.
