## Overview & Synopsis

### Official Synopsis
The X-Men’s epic final battle is packed with nonstop action, amazing special effects, and all-new heroes and villains. When a controversial “cure” is discovered for mutants, peaceful leader Charles Xavier (Patrick Stewart) clashes with his militant counterpart, Magneto (Ian McKellen), triggering the war to end all wars!

As Jean Grey (Famke Janssen) resurrects as the powerful, uncontrollable Dark Phoenix, Wolverine (Hugh Jackman), Storm (Halle Berry), Rogue (Anna Paquin), and the rest of the X-Men must unite to face the ultimate choice: preserve their mutant heritage or give up their powers to become human.
