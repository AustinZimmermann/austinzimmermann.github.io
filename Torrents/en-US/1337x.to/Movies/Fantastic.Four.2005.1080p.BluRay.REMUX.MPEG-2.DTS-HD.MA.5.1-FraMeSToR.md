## Plot Summary

Inventive scientist Reed Richards convinces corrupt corporate billionaire Victor von Doom to fund his experimental space mission to study a cosmic energy storm. Accompanied by his astronaut pilot Ben Grimm, his corporate rival and ex-girlfriend Susan Storm, and her reckless hot-shot brother Johnny Storm, the team launches into space. 

However, Reed miscalculates the speed of the storm. The cosmic cloud overtakes their space station, bombarding them with mutagenic radiation. Upon returning to Earth, they discover their DNA has been radically altered, granting them extraordinary superhuman abilities:

* **Reed Richards** gains the ability to stretch, deform, and elongate his body into any shape.
* **Susan Storm** can render herself entirely invisible and project powerful invisible force fields.
* **Johnny Storm** can engulf his entire body in plasma fire, fly at hypersonic speeds, and manipulate thermal energy.
* **Ben Grimm** is transformed into a massive, rocky behemoth possessing superhuman strength, dense stone skin, and incredible durability.

As they struggle to cope with their newly transformed lives and public celebrity status, Victor von Doom—who suffered severe financial ruin and physical scarring from the exposure—begins plotting to eliminate the Fantastic Four and harness their powers to conquer the world as Doctor Doom.
