## Movie Synopsis

For decades, Wreck-It Ralph (voiced by John C. He embarks on a game-hopping journey through multiple generations of video games to prove he’s got what it takes to be a hero. Along the way, he sneaks into *Hero’s Duty*, a gritty first-person shooter, and accidentally unleashes a deadly cyber-netic menace that threatens every arcade game in the establishment. His quest ultimately leads him to *Sugar Rush*, a vibrant kart-racing game where he encounters Vanellope von Schweetz (voiced by Sarah Silverman), a young “glitch” misfit who teaches him the true meaning of friendship and heroism.
